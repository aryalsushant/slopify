# Aldridge & Vance — funeral directors

Fourth-generation funeral home in Shropshire, established 1897. Burials,
cremations, and repatriation. We hold the licence for the chapel of rest on
Bishop Street and we do most of the county's Jewish and Muslim funerals, which
means same-day arrangements matter.

The site is read by people in the worst week of their life, usually on a phone,
often at three in the morning. It needs the out-of-hours number above everything
else, then what to do in the first 24 hours after a death, then how much things
cost, plainly, with no upselling.

Tone: plain, calm, unornamented. No stock photography of sunsets or hands. No
marketing language of any kind. Never the word "celebration".
