# OFF-REGISTER — punk zine, issue 12

Photocopied quarterly zine out of a basement in Leeds. Riso and toner, stapled
by hand, 200 copies a run. Issue 12 is about squatted venues that got shut down
between 1994 and 2003, with interviews and a lot of flyer scans.

The site is a distribution page, not a brand. It needs the issue index, a mail
order form, and the list of record shops and infoshops that stock us. Prices in
pounds, postage separate.

Look: xeroxed, misregistered, high-contrast halftone. Cut-and-paste ransom-note
headlines. Nothing centred, nothing rounded, nothing that looks designed by
committee. If it looks like a startup we have failed.
