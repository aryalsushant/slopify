# Hollowback Trust — river restoration charity

Registered charity restoring chalk streams in Hampshire. We remove redundant
weirs, replant marginal reedbed, and run a volunteer riverfly monitoring scheme
with about ninety recorders across eleven catchments.

The site has three jobs: recruit recorders, take donations, and publish the
annual invertebrate survey data so the water company cannot dispute it. The
survey data is the whole reason we have leverage, so it goes on the site raw,
with methodology, not summarised into a graphic.

We are legally obliged to publish trustee names and our Charity Commission
number. Please do not invent any figures — every number on the site has to trace
to the survey or to the accounts.

Tone: sober, evidential, a bit stubborn.
