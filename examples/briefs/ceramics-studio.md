# Soroe Ceramics — studio site

A two-potter studio in coastal Maine. We throw functional stoneware on the wheel
and fire everything in a wood-fired anagama kiln twice a year. Every piece is
hand-thrown, so no two glazes come out the same, and that variation is the whole
point of buying from us rather than from a shop.

The site needs to carry the kiln. The firing takes four days and three people
feeding cordwood around the clock, and the ash deposits are what give the shino
glaze its colour. That story is why people pay what they pay.

We sell three ways: seasonal kiln-opening sales, a small permanent collection of
tumblers and serving bowls, and commissions for restaurants.

Tone: quiet, earthy, unhurried. Absolutely no gradients.
